About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Mathematics at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILAB), Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro.

My research lies at the intersection of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI), Computational Intelligence, and Healthcare / Digital Medicine. In particular, I investigate how fuzzy logic and linguistic representations can render unsupervised acoustic models transparent and clinically interpretable for speech analysis in patients affected by neurodegenerative disorders, such as Parkinson's disease.

Research interests

  • Explainable AI (XAI): Fuzzy linguistic representations, interpretable machine learning, human-in-the-loop AI.
  • Biomedical AI & Voice Analysis: Acoustic patterns, vocal biomarkers, clinical decision-support systems.
  • Computational Intelligence: Fuzzy sets and systems, unsupervised clustering, pattern recognition.
